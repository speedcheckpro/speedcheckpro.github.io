Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

SpeedCheck Pro is a free information website. This policy explains what information is collected when you use it, and what we do with it. In short: we collect as little as possible, and we never sell personal information.

Information We Do Not Collect

Using the speed test does not require an account, a name, an email address, or any other personal information. Your speed test results are measured in your own browser and are not sent to us or stored by us. We do not ask you to install anything, and we do not track you across other websites.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic technical information in server logs when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit, and the time of your visit. This is used only for operating the site, keeping it secure, and understanding overall usage in aggregate. It is not used to identify individual visitors.

Speed Test Data

The speed test exchanges test data with a third-party test server (operated by Cloudflare) in order to measure your connection. That traffic is limited to the test itself — downloading and uploading blocks of test data and timing them. The test server operator may process the technical data needed to serve those requests, under its own privacy policy. No personal information is included in the test data.

Cookies and Analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies. If analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated to describe what is collected and how to opt out.

Contacting Us

If you email us, we will use your email address only to reply to your message. We will not add you to a mailing list, share your address with anyone, or use it for any other purpose.

Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us.
