Speed Test Results

Speed test results are measurements of your connection at the moment of the test, taken in your browser under the conditions at that time. Results can vary between tests for many reasons — Wi-Fi signal, other devices on your network, the time of day, your device, and the route to the test server. A single result is a snapshot, not a guarantee of the speed you will get at any other time, and it is not proof of the speed your internet provider is delivering to your home.

We show only real measured figures and never estimate or inflate results, but we cannot guarantee that any measurement is exact, complete, or free from error. If you need a formal record of your connection speed for a dispute with a provider, use your provider's own testing process or an independent measurement method they accept.
