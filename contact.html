Contact Us

We would like to hear from you — whether you have a question about a speed test result, spotted a mistake in one of our guides, or have an idea for a guide we should write next.

Email

The best way to reach us is by email: contact@speedcheckpro.github.io

Please include as much detail as you can. If your message is about a speed test result, tell us your download and upload speeds, your ping, whether you tested over Wi-Fi or a cable, and what type of connection you have — that helps us give you a useful answer instead of a guess.

What to Expect

We read every message and aim to reply within a few working days. Please note that we cannot fix your internet connection remotely, contact your provider on your behalf, or see your test results — results are measured in your own browser and are never sent to us.

Before You Write

Many common questions are already answered on this site. If your connection feels slow, start with our guide to how to fix slow internet; if you are unsure what your numbers mean, see what a good internet speed is.
