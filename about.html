About SpeedCheck Pro

SpeedCheck Pro is a free, independent website built for one simple job: helping people understand how fast their internet really is — and what to do about it if it is not fast enough.

Most speed test sites give you three numbers and leave you to figure out the rest. We think the numbers are only half the story. That is why every test on this site sits alongside plain-English guides that explain what ping, download and upload speeds actually mean, how much speed different activities need, and how to fix a slow connection without paying for an upgrade you do not need.

How Our Speed Test Works

When you run a test, your browser measures your connection directly: it sends small requests to time your ping, downloads test data in parallel streams to measure download speed, and uploads test data to measure upload speed. Every figure shown is a real measurement taken at that moment. We never show estimated, guessed or placeholder speeds, and we never inflate a result to make a connection look better than it is.

The test runs entirely in your browser. You do not need to create an account, install an app, or give us any personal information to use it.

What We Publish

  • Free tools — a speed test, a ping test, a download time calculator and a speed requirements checker.
  • Guides — twenty original articles covering good speeds, Wi-Fi, gaming, streaming, working from home and more.
  • Honest advice — if a problem can be fixed for free, we say so. If a plan upgrade is genuinely the answer, we say that too.
