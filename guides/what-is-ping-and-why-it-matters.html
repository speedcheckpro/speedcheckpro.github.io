What Is Ping and Why It Matters

SpeedCheck Pro Guide • Updated November 2026 • 5 min read

Ping is the time it takes for a small packet of data to travel from your device to a server and back again. It is measured in milliseconds, written as ms, and a lower number is always better. While download speed tells you how much data your connection can move, ping tells you how quickly it starts moving. A connection can be fast on paper and still feel slow if the ping is high, because every click, every word on a video call, and every move in a game has to wait for that round trip.

What does ping actually feel like?

At 20 ms, everything feels instant: web pages respond the moment you click, video call conversations flow naturally, and online games react as fast as you do. At 100 ms, you start to notice a slight delay. People talk over each other on calls, and in fast games your character reacts a fraction of a second late. At 200 ms or more, the delay is obvious: calls become awkward, with long pauses before the other person answers, and competitive games become frustrating because what you see on screen happened a while ago on the server. This is why gamers care about ping far more than raw speed. A 50 Mbps connection with 15 ms ping will feel better in a game than a 500 Mbps connection with 150 ms ping.

What is a good ping?

Good ping depends on what you are doing, but the ranges below are a useful guide for most people.

PingWhat it feels like
Under 20 msExcellent: ideal for competitive gaming and video calls
20 to 50 msGood: smooth gaming, calls, and browsing
50 to 100 msFair: fine for browsing and streaming, noticeable in fast games
Over 100 msPoor: lag in games, delays on calls, sluggish feel

Your ping also depends on distance. A server in your own country will almost always give you a lower ping than a server on another continent, because the signal has further to travel. That is one reason games let you pick a server region: the closest region usually gives the best ping.

Ping vs speed: what is the difference?

Speed and ping measure two different things. Think of your connection as a road. Download speed is how wide the road is, how many cars can travel at once. Ping is how long one car takes to drive to the other end and back. A very wide road does not help if the journey itself is slow, and a quick journey does not help if the road is too narrow for all the traffic. You need both: enough speed for your activities, and low enough ping for them to feel responsive. Our guide to what a good internet speed is covers the speed side of this in detail.

What causes high ping?

Several things can push your ping up. A weak Wi-Fi signal is one of the most common: if your device is far from the router or separated by thick walls, packets get delayed or lost and have to be sent again. A busy home network is another: if someone is downloading a large file or backing up photos to the cloud while you play, your ping will climb. The type of connection matters too. Fibre and cable usually give lower ping than DSL or satellite, and satellite is the worst of all because the signal travels to space and back. Finally, the server you connect to matters, as does the route your data takes through the internet, which you cannot control directly.

How to lower your ping

Start with the easy fixes. Use a wired ethernet cable instead of Wi-Fi for gaming and important calls: it removes wireless interference and usually cuts ping straight away. Move closer to your router, or move the router to a more central spot. Close downloads, cloud backups, and streaming on other devices while you play. Restart your router, which clears stale connections. If the problem is the game server, try a different server region. For a full step-by-step plan, see our guide to how to lower ping in games, and our explainer on jitter and packet loss, two related problems that often appear alongside high ping.

Tip: Always test your ping on a wired connection before blaming your provider. If wired ping is low but Wi-Fi ping is high, the fix is your Wi-Fi setup, not a faster plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good ping for online gaming?

Under 50 ms is good for most online games, and under 20 ms is excellent for fast competitive games. Above 100 ms you will notice real lag. If your ping is high, a wired connection and a closer game server usually help more than a faster plan.

Can I have fast internet but high ping?

Yes. Download speed and ping are separate measurements. A 500 Mbps plan can still have 150 ms ping if the Wi-Fi is weak, the server is far away, or the network is congested. Speed is about capacity; ping is about delay.

Does ping affect video calls and streaming?

Ping affects video calls a lot: high ping causes awkward pauses and people talking over each other. It barely affects streaming, because video players buffer several seconds ahead, so a slow start is the only thing you notice.
